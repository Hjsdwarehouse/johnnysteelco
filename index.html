01 Cost

Where the money actually goes

Most steel building projects do not fail in fabrication. They fail in the first three decisions.

Span and columns

Every metre of clear span you ask for is bought with steel. So is every column you remove. There is a point where the honest answer is “keep a column”, and it is often earlier than expected.

What the building must carry

A crane, a mezzanine, a silo, a suspended conveyor. Each one changes the frame before the frame exists. Adding them later is not a detail change, it is a new building.

The environment it sits in

Coastal air, wet processing floors, fertiliser dust, abrasive aggregate. The same shed lasts fifteen years in one place and five in another. This is decided at specification, not at maintenance.
